Questions

Is there a commission?

No. Payments to state bodies and public utilities through the gateway and at Talasbank branches are free of commission. Irad Pochtasy charges its own fee.

Why only Talasbank cards?

Talasbank keeps the accounts of the state bodies listed on e-Irad. Payments by cards of other banks would pass through the interbank clearing of the Bank of Irad and are not yet provided for.

Can I pay with Tulpar Pay?

No. Tulpar Pay is not accepted for state services.

Can I pay in dollars?

Payments to state bodies are accepted in som only. Dollars can be exchanged at the official rate of the Bank of Irad at any Talasbank branch. bank.iradgov.org

I paid the wrong account.

Write an application at the branch where you paid, with the receipt and your ID card. The money is returned after the recipient confirms, usually within 30 days.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Payments through the gateway need no sign-in. When My e-Irad opens, your payments will be listed there after you sign in with e-Irad ID: id.e-irad.org
